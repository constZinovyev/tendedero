# Seagull calls

All clips are one bird, cut from a single recording of a herring gull
(*Larus argentatus*) on Wikimedia Commons, then filtered, faded and levelled:
[Gull 2.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gull_2.ogg), avphillips,
Damariscotta Falls, Maine, April 1998. Public domain.

Keep to this one recording: clips from other birds make it sound like a flock.

## The clips, in seconds of Gull 2.ogg

Approved by ear (2026-10-10). Filter: two 700 Hz high-passes, afftdn
(nf -25, nr 18), short fades, loudnorm I -18 / TP -2; AAC 96k mono.
