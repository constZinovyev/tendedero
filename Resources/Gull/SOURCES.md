# Seagull calls

Short clips cut from recordings of herring gulls (*Larus argentatus*) on
Wikimedia Commons, filtered, faded and levelled:

- `call-1`…`call-4`, `long-1`, `alarm-1`: [Gull 1.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gull_1.ogg), avphillips, Long Island, 1999. Public domain.
- `call-5`, `long-2`, `alarm-2`: [Gull 2.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gull_2.ogg), avphillips, Damariscotta Falls, Maine, 1998. Public domain.
- `call-6`…`call-8`, `flight-1`…`flight-4`: [XC707075 European Herring Gull](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:XC707075_-_European_Herring_Gull_-_Larus_argentatus.mp3), Sonothèque ADVL (xeno-canto XC707075). CC0.
